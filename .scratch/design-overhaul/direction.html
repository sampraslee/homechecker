Home Check: visual direction, take 2

This version is rebuilt from rizurf.com: its teal, its slate greys, its system font and its dark hero. The one bold element is a dark band at the top of each screen, with the site's grid, teal glow and teal-to-mint edge. The rest is light, because the app is used in bright rooms. Colours that are too light for text on rizurf.com have deeper versions here.

A-12-3, Residensi Harmoni (monospace, Unit line only)

  • Same font as the website: San Francisco on iPhone, Roboto on Android. There is nothing to download, so it works offline, and it is what the phone already reads best. Headlines are bold with tight spacing, as on the site.
  • Monospace in one place only: the Unit line in the top band, as a nod to the site's monospace labels. It keeps 0 and O, and 1 and l, apart in unit numbers.
  • Teal for actions, blue for AI. Teal is Rizurf's main colour, so it marks everything the Inspector does. Blue, from homefinderpro, marks only what the AI suggests.
  • The site's light greys are too faint for glare. Card and input edges use a darker slate (3.1:1) instead of the site's slate-200 (1.2:1).
  • Radius as on the site: 6px for buttons, inputs and photos, 12px for cards, and pills only for buttons in the dark band.
  • Tap targets are 48px or more; the main button is 56px. Body text is 17px.
