# 05: Video and note Evidence

**What to build:** On an Item, the Inspector records a video with sound that stops automatically at 30 seconds, or adds a text note. Videos are kept at the phone's normal quality with a generated thumbnail; they are not compressed and not sent to AI. Videos and notes can be linked to Defects like photos. See the spec: Evidence.

**Blocked by:** 03

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [x] Record video with audio via MediaRecorder; recording stops at 30 seconds
- [x] Original video stored unchanged; thumbnail generated by the Image processor adapter; duration recorded
- [x] Add, edit and delete a text note on an Item (the "while Draft" check is deferred to 06)
- [x] Videos and notes appear on the Item alongside photos and can be linked to that Item's Defects
- [x] Videos and notes are never placed in the AI queue (there is no queue until 07; see the comment there)
- [x] Delete a video (the "only while Draft" check is deferred to 06, like photos)
- [x] Core tests cover the 30 s cap metadata and linking (Draft-only deletion is tested in 06)

## Comments

- From 03: `Evidence` is currently `kind: "photo"` with a photo-only `processed` flag, and media is keyed by `PhotoVersion` (original / evidenceCopy / thumbnail). Videos and notes will need `Evidence` to become a discriminated union by kind. Video needs a duration and only original + thumbnail; a note needs text and no media. The Item screen also renders every piece of Evidence as a photo.
- `Evidence` is now `PhotoEvidence | VideoEvidence | NoteEvidence` (`MediaEvidence` = photo or video). `PhotoVersion` became `MediaVersion`, `getPhoto` became `getMedia`, and `finishPhotoProcessing` became `finishMediaProcessing` (it also retries video thumbnails). A video has `processed` like a photo; a note has neither media nor `processed`.
- The core owns the limit: `VIDEO_TIME_LIMIT_SECONDS` (30) is exported for the recorder's timer. `addVideo(itemId, original, durationSeconds)` refuses more than 31 s (`video-too-long`): the recorder's stop lands a moment after 30 s, so up to 1 s over is stored as 30. Durations are stored to 0.1 s.
- The recorder measures duration from the recorder's start to the moment the stop is asked for, not from the video file: Chrome's MediaRecorder WebM files report no duration.
- The video thumbnail is drawn after seeking to the start (`seeked`), not on `loadeddata`: iOS fires `loadeddata` before a frame is painted, which would give a black thumbnail. If no frame arrives within 10 s, the video is kept without a thumbnail and retried at app start.
- A video without a thumbnail shows a dark tile with its length. Unlike a photo, its original is never used as the thumbnail, because it isn't an image.
- Notes: `addNote` / `editNote` trim the text and refuse blank (`blank-note`, distinct from a Defect's `note-required`); `editNote` on a photo or video is refused (`evidence-not-a-note`). Notes are deleted with `deleteEvidence`, which unlinks them from Defects like photos.
- The recorder needs a secure context: over plain HTTP (the LAN dev server) it shows "Recording video needs the app opened from its https:// link." instead of a camera.
- Checked in the desktop browser with a canvas stream in place of the camera: a 2.5 s recording, and one that stopped itself at 30 s and was stored as 30. Not yet checked on a real phone's camera and microphone (iOS Safari records MP4, Chrome WebM); 14 covers that.
