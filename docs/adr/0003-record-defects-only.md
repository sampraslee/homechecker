# Inspections record Defects only, not good condition

Even though Home Check serves move-in and move-out tenancy records, an Inspection records only Defects. There is no required overview Evidence per Area and no "no defects" mark per Item. This keeps inspections fast; the accepted cost is that a Defect found at Move-out cannot be proven absent at Move-in, only shown as not recorded.

## Considered Options

- Required overview photos or video per Area (proves good condition at little cost to the Inspector)
- Marking every Item as "no defects" or "has defects" (strongest proof, slowest inspection)
